CORS proxy

YouTube and many other hosts do not allow browsers to read their feeds. By default https://corsproxy.io is used. Enter the URL prefix of a CORS proxy you trust instead (the feed URL is appended URL-encoded), or clear the field to disable it. It is only used when a direct request fails.
